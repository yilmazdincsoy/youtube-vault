YouTube Vault

A personal tool that turns my liked YouTube videos into a searchable Obsidian notes archive.

What it does

  • Reads the list of videos I have liked on YouTube (read-only access via the YouTube Data API).
  • Classifies each video by topic using an AI classification model.
  • Writes one Markdown note per video into a local Obsidian vault on my own computer.
  • Lets me search my liked videos in natural language.

Who it is for

This is a personal, non-commercial project used only by its developer. It is not offered as a service to other users.

Privacy

See the Privacy Policy for how YouTube data is accessed, used and stored.
