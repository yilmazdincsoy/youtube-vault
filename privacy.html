Privacy Policy

YouTube Vault · Last updated: October 4, 2026

YouTube Vault is a personal, non-commercial tool used only by its developer. This policy explains how it accesses, uses and stores data obtained through Google APIs.

Data accessed

With the user's explicit consent, the app requests the read-only scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly and reads only the list of videos the user has liked, together with public video metadata (title, channel name, description, tags, category and duration). The app cannot like, upload, delete, comment on or otherwise change anything on the user's YouTube account.

How the data is used

The data is used solely to build a personal, searchable notes archive of the user's liked videos: classifying each video by topic and allowing natural-language search over the archive.

Storage

All data, including the OAuth access token, is stored locally on the user's own computer. The app has no server and no database of its own.

Sharing with third parties

To classify videos and answer searches, the public metadata of liked videos (title, channel, description, tags) is sent to the TypeSafe AI API (typesafe.ai), an AI classification service. No account credentials, tokens or personal identifiers are sent. The data is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone else.

Compliance

YouTube Vault's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube data is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's local data folder removes all stored data.
